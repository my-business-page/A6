## Site map
Home / Menu / Our Story / How It Works / Order & Pickup / Schedule / FAQ / Contact.

## Visual
Premium Japanese-neighbourhood tea aesthetic: off-white, dark cocoa, matcha, muted terracotta. Strong vertical typography and restrained Japanese graphic motifs.

## Motion/VFX
Whisk-stroke SVG animation; floating tea dust; drink layers assemble on scroll; menu cards lift subtly; schedule tiles animate into place; smooth route transitions. Scroll entering/exiting must use opacity/blur/translate and reverse on upward scrolling.

## Functional
Owner-editable schedule; preorder CTA; pickup privacy logic; menu sold-out states; PayNow instructions only if still current; Instagram/Telegram; FAQ; accessible and fast.

## Accuracy rule
Do not copy old hours from the public info page as current. Build the schedule as editable data and verify before launch.

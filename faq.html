07 / FAQ

Before you
visit.

Is Horikawa a regular café?

No. It is a home-based matcha and coffee window with session-based self-collection.

Where do I collect?

Self-collection is in Yew Tee, Singapore 680784. The exact address is shared after order confirmation.

Are drinks hot?

The public information currently states that all drinks are served iced.

Do drinks contain allergens?

Some items contain nuts or dairy. Public information says the items do not contain pork or lard. Check the live menu for each product.

Can I walk in?

Use the preorder/session flow first; Horikawa is a home-based window rather than a conventional café.

03 / Our story

Two sisters.
One neighbourhood.

Horikawa is run by two sisters who have lived in Yew Tee their entire lives. They loved visiting cafes and making their own matcha and coffee, then decided to bring those drinks closer to the neighbourhood.

The result is intentionally small: a home-based window, iced drinks, preorder sessions and a community-first rhythm.

“brewing joy in the neighbourhood ☕”
